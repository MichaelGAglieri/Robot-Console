# DOC five-axis model

`doc5.urdf` describes the five-axis D.O.C. arm and its three-finger gripper. Joint
frames, link lengths and the placement of every part come from the assembly of the
ToolBox Robotics **EB-15** arm (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6488872) and
**EBG-30** gripper (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6460300) carried out by
Michael Giuseppe Aglieri for the D.O.C. project.

## Bundled meshes: schematic, GPL-3.0-only

The meshes in `meshes/` are simple capsules, discs and boxes generated by
`scripts/schematic_model.py` from the sizes in `schematic.json`. They contain no
ToolBox Robotics geometry.

## Detailed meshes: generated on your computer

The ToolBox STL parts are licensed **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. The merged and simplified
display meshes are an adaptation, so they are not distributed. To see the detailed
model, download the two packages from Thingiverse and choose **Studio → Import EB-15
parts…** (or run `python3 eb15_model.py EB-15.zip EBG-30.zip`). Robot Console
recognises the original parts by SHA-256 (`eb15_assembly.json`, GPL placement data
only) and writes the meshes into your own data folder, for personal non-commercial
use. Do not share the generated files.

Joint zeros (arm straight up) and link lengths come from the CAD parts, not from
measurements of the physical robot. Mass and inertia are not included.
