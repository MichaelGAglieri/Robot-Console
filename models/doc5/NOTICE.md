# DOC five-axis model (EB-15 parts)

Geometry: original STL parts of the ToolBox Robotics **EB-15** collaborative arm
(https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6488872) and **EBG-30** gripper
(https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6460300), by ToolBoxRobotics, licensed
**CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. Parts are unmodified in shape; they are positioned per the
ToolBox assembly drawings, merged per link and colour, and simplified by vertex
clustering (0.8 mm) for display. The sixth EB-15 axis (parts 021–023) is omitted
because the physical D.O.C. has five axes.

Assembly, joint frames and URDF: Michael Giuseppe Aglieri (D.O.C. project).
Generator: D.O.C.Mac/hardware/modello_doc5/genera_modello.py.

Joint zeros (arm straight up) and link lengths come from the CAD parts, not from
measurements of the physical robot. Mass and inertia are not included.
Because of the NonCommercial/NoDerivatives terms, check the licence before
redistributing these meshes outside personal, non-commercial use.
